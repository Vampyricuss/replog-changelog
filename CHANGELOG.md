# RepLog Changelog

What's new in RepLog, newest first. RepLog is free with nothing behind a paywall.

## October 2026
- **Muscle split radar chart.** An alternative to the muscle map on the Stats tab: sets per muscle group against your weekly targets. Choose Map, Radar or Both in Settings.
- **Share a workout from History.** Open any past workout and tap the share icon for the same share card you get after finishing.
- **Siri Shortcut.** Say "Start my next workout in RepLog" to jump straight into your next routine.
- **Bolder look.** Glowing exercise icons, bigger and chunkier reps and weight on the set you're on, and colour-coded Settings icons.
- **Tap a set to complete it.** Tap anywhere on the row to tick it off; hold it to edit or remove.
- **Faster start.** Your routines, exercises and history show instantly from your phone while the latest data loads, instead of flashing empty.
- **Friendlier errors.** Technical error text from the server is replaced with a plain message.
- **Fix:** water reminders no longer fire in a burst during a workout.

## September 2026
- **Progress photos removed.** The feature is gone for now to keep storage costs down. Your other data is unaffected.
- **Plan a run.** A quick action that jumps straight to a running set, with an optional route planner for distance (no location access needed).
- **Timers for timed and distance sets.** Start, pause, adjust by 15 seconds and finish early.
- **Daily workout reminder.** An optional notification, plus a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island banner naming your next routine.
- **Over 600 exercises.** 503 new exercises with illustrations, instructions and exact muscles worked.
- **Nutrition tab (optional).** Water tracking with a goal, drink reminders and a seven-day chart, plus food logging with calories and macros. Off unless you turn it on.
- **Rest timer improvements.** Sounds when the rest ends, a countdown ring in the Dynamic Island, and it re-syncs correctly when you return to the app.
- **Starter plans, routine sharing and import.** Add a ready-made plan from Explore, share a routine as text, and paste one in to import it.
- **Accessibility pass.** VoiceOver labels, Move up and Move down actions for reordering, and an accessibility page.
- **Support RepLog tip jar.** Optional tips that unlock nothing.
